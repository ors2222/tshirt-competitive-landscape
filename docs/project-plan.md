## Current decisions and open questions

- The focus is a plain white T-shirt, with the name **Blank Theory** still a working concept.
- The primary creative wearer and secondary child/activity/gift audiences are hypotheses, not validated segments.
- The desired look is described as relaxed with a boxy silhouette, but the exact garment and dimensions are unknown.
- The learner's postmodernist-leaning hero choice is an existing project decision; wordmark and palette remain proposals pending review.
- The current comparison is dated desk research. Price snapshots are not like-for-like and the purchase flows were not completed.
- Paint/marker suitability, product fit, customer demand, and brand effects are not established.

## Revisions

Update `revisions.md` when a material page, source, or decision changes. Record what changed, why, the commit, and any limitations. Keep old research dates visible rather than silently replacing snapshots.
