## What the research does and does not say

**Observed:** page text, controls, displayed price snapshots, available size or design tools, and where each observed buying path stopped.

**Interpretation:** archetypes, design language, and possible persuasion cues are readings of the observed pages. They are not the companies' stated strategy, proof of customer motivation, or proof that a cue increased sales.

**Not measured:** market share, total addressable market, customer satisfaction, conversion, brand awareness, purchase intent, product quality, paint performance, or checkout completion.

Prices, ratings, availability, and page details change. The values above belong to dated captures, not current quotes or like-for-like comparisons.

## Practical gap to explore

The strongest opportunity is not a bare “white shirt as canvas” slogan: Custom Ink already makes customization explicit, and Michaels offers a blank. The more useful question is whether Blank Theory can make choosing and using this exact shirt clearer for creative people.

That could mean a trustworthy product page with actual garment views, verified fit and care details, a simple guide to creative options, and clear boundaries about what is included. Paint or marker compatibility must be tested before any such claim appears.
