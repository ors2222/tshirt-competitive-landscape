## Method and limits

The source comparison records observed page content, controls, price snapshots, and where attempted buying paths stopped. It is a small desk-research sample from November 3, 2026, not a market-size estimate, customer study, product test, or controlled competitor analysis. No purchase was made and no order was completed. Some checkout screens and prefilled fields were not submitted. Captured details can change.

The related source report and screenshot records are in the separate Brand Studio repository's `feature/brand-001` branch. This review repository summarizes the useful findings and links to the source; it does not republish those screenshot assets.
