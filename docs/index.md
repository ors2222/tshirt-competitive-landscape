## What this board is for

This is a review space for the plain white T-shirt project: what the existing competitor research actually observed, what it might mean for **Blank Theory**, and what should be checked before the product page makes a promise.

The source report is dated **November 3, 2026**. Prices are snapshots from different products and purchase paths, not a controlled price comparison. No order was placed. The proposed audience and brand direction remain hypotheses.

<div class="callout">
<strong>Current read:</strong> “A blank you can make your own” is not enough by itself. Custom Ink already foregrounds customization, while Michaels offers a low-cost blank. A stronger, supportable difference could be a clearer creative-use guide and more trustworthy product information—but only after the actual shirt and use case are checked.
</div>

## The short version

| Buyer question | What the observed sites help with | What Blank Theory still needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is this an everyday shirt? | UNIQLO presents a ready-to-wear basic with sizes, imagery, and retail controls. | Identify the actual blank, fabric, fit, care, and measurements. |
| Is it a low-cost blank for a project? | Michaels showed a Gildan tee at $3.29 in a size-L cart snapshot. | Decide whether the offer includes only the shirt or anything else. |
| Can I customize the garment? | Custom Ink provides a design and group-order workflow; its captured price began at $7.75 per item. | Decide if the brand sells a blank, a design service, or a validated creative-use experience. |
| Can I paint or write on it? | The current comparison does not prove paint compatibility for the intended shirt. | Verify fabric, materials, wash behavior, and care before saying yes. |
